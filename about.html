About Us

Starline Logistics was founded in 2020 from a simple belief that reliable, efficient transport should not be complicated.Based in Durban, we specialise in the safe and timely movement of cargo,with a key focus on the Johanesburgto Durban corridor.Ourteam operates with integrity,professionalism,and a commitment to consistent service delivery.
